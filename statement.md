# CareMate

## 1. Problem Statement

People with busy schedules often find it difficult to keep track of their daily tasks, expenses, personal care, and health-related reminders. Students and working professionals may have multiple responsibilities and may forget important tasks or personal records because of their workload.

CareMate aims to provide a simple, menu-driven Python application that brings these basic management features together in one place.

## 2. Scope of the Project

The project focuses on providing basic tools for managing daily tasks, expenses, care reminders, and personal records. It is designed as a simple Python application that can be used without external packages or complex setup.

The current project stores information while the program is running. Advanced features such as permanent data storage, online accounts, and graphical interfaces are outside the current scope.

## 3. Target Users

CareMate is designed for a general audience, especially:

* Students who have difficulty keeping track of their academic and personal tasks because of workload.
* Working professionals who have busy schedules and need help managing tasks, expenses, and personal reminders.
* Other individuals who want a simple way to organize daily responsibilities and personal care records.

## 4. High-Level Features

* Task management
* Task priority and completion tracking
* Expense management and total expense calculation
* Care reminders
* Optional period tracker
* Simple dashboard for viewing a summary of records
